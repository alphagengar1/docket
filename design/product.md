# Product: one-person credit union

One human runs a compliant lending desk through an org chart of agents.

1. Applicant chats with the Intake agent: consent screen, loan purpose, amount, self-reported income (CRS has no income data).
2. Backend pulls live CRS sandbox data on the chosen test persona: soft-pull prequal score, identity verification, fraud signals.
3. Three Underwriter agents, each with an explicit policy (conservative credit union, growth fintech, prime bank), return an offer or a decline with reasons.
4. Compliance agent writes the adverse-action notice in plain English for any decline.
5. Offers stream onto the live board. The human loan officer approves.

Judge draws the applicant from a printed deck of persona cards. Fraud / deceased / frozen personas hard-stop with zero offers and an explanation.

Tracks: CRS, Hyphenate (finance seats: underwriter + compliance, human approver, planted errors), Convex, Microsoft, Anthropic, general. CREAO only if eligibility is confirmed (decision Sat 12:30).

Pitch line: "A loan officer gets one lender's yes or no with no reasons. We let one person run a whole lending desk: three underwriting policies compete on verified credit data, the legally required adverse-action notice writes itself, and you pick the applicant."

Do not call it a marketplace. All lenders are ours.
