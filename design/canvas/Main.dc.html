Five rules
Color

Gray means normal. Color means something happened.

Orange is the only alarm. Blue is the only go. Everything idle stays gray, so the one red moment owns the screen.

Distance

The board is read from 2 m away.

On the 1440 board, nothing a judge must read is under 22px. States and key numbers are 44px or more. Detail lives on the laptop.

Offers

Rank by cost to borrow, not by payment.

Each offer shows APR, monthly payment and cost to borrow: what you repay minus the cash you get. Fees go into the cost.

Codes

Four digits, one field, the number pad.

Digits open the number pad. One input keeps paste and delete simple. The phone sends at the fourth digit.

Cards

The code sits in the corners.

Like playing-card corner indices: the number and code read in a fanned hand, from either end.
